# Image Processing Coursework

This repository contains my assignments, implementations, experiments, and study notes developed as part of the **Image Processing** course in the **Master's Degree in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at the Universidad de Guadalajara**.

## About this repository

This repository documents my learning process throughout the course and includes practical implementations of the concepts covered in class.

The main purpose is to strengthen my understanding of digital image processing by combining theoretical concepts with hands-on programming, experimentation, visualization, and analysis.

The contents may include:

* Image manipulation and visualization.
* Image enhancement techniques.
* Spatial and frequency domain processing.
* Filtering and convolution.
* Histogram analysis and transformations.
* Noise reduction.
* Edge and feature detection.
* Image segmentation.
* Morphological operations.
* Programming assignments and experiments.
* Personal notes and observations related to the course topics.

## Learning Objectives

Through this repository, I aim to:

* Strengthen my understanding of the mathematical foundations of digital image processing.
* Implement fundamental image processing algorithms from scratch when appropriate.
* Understand how images can be represented, transformed, filtered, and analyzed computationally.
* Explore spatial and frequency domain techniques.
* Analyze the effects of different image processing algorithms through practical experiments.
* Develop a stronger foundation for future work in computer vision and artificial intelligence.
* Build a structured record of my progress throughout the course.

## Repository Structure

The repository will be organized according to the topics, assignments, and exercises covered during the course.

Each folder or notebook may contain:

* The problem or image processing concept being studied.
* The corresponding implementation.
* Input images and datasets when appropriate.
* Experiments and visual results.
* Analysis and conclusions derived from the exercise.

The structure may evolve as new topics are introduced throughout the semester.

## Technologies
